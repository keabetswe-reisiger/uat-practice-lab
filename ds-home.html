In plain words
This stage teaches data science in twelve modules, in a sensible order. Each module has short lessons, practice on a pretend coffee business, and a ten-question checkpoint that you pass at 80%. Work through them in order, or jump to the one you need. The SQL module uses the SQL lessons that are already in this app.
Coffee analogy
Think of it as training a new barista in stages: first how the shop works, then the till, then the machine, then ordering and the numbers, and finally running a shift alone. Each stage ends with a short test before the next one.
Worked example
You finish module 1 (4 lessons, 2 exercises, 1 checkpoint = 7 steps). After 2 lessons and 1 exercise you have 3 of 7 steps, so the bar shows 43%. Passing the checkpoint with 8 of 10 is the last step.
Words to know
module
A group of 3 to 5 lessons on one topic, with practice and a checkpoint.
exercise
A small question on the practice data, checked by the page.
checkpoint
Ten questions at the end of a module. 8 correct passes it.
