Research notes

What was looked up to build this stage, what the sources said, and how far each point can be trusted. Research was done on 10 November 2026 using web searches. Result pages were returned by the search tool, and most were not opened in full, so "Verified" here means an official or well-known page said it directly. Always check the official page before you pay for an exam or course.

Legend. Verified: stated on an official page in the results. Indicative: stated by training or career sites that sell courses, so treat it as a rough guide. Unverified: found in one weak source, or not found at all.

The skills map

Guides agree that the core of entry-level data science is Python, SQL, statistics and probability, and machine learning, usually learned in that order or with SQL first. Deep learning is described as not required for most entry-level roles. MLOps, cloud basics and generative AI are growing extras. One guide quotes Python in over 95% and SQL in over 80% of data scientist job adverts, but the source is a training vendor, so that is indicative. This shaped the lesson order: tables, statistics, SQL, Python, cleaning, inference, then machine learning.

Roles

IBM and several career sites split the field into analyst (describes data, often reports and charts), scientist (statistics and models to predict), engineer (pipelines and data movement) and machine learning engineer (usually from software engineering, putting models into products). Sources stress that the roles overlap and titles vary. Status: indicative, because most sources are informal articles.

Passive income

Sources were mostly marketing blogs. Reported ranges: template shops from tens to a couple of thousand a month after 3 to 12 months; new courses roughly $50 to $500 a month, with one source claiming a median instructor income under $500 a year; a data author arguing the data science course and ebook market is saturated. No reliable figures were found for selling datasets or for freelance rates. Status: indicative at best. The key lesson from every source: passive income needs a large upfront effort and is not guaranteed.

South Africa

Sources describe positive demand for data roles in finance, telecoms and retail, but salary figures conflict by a wide margin (a monthly figure near R23 000 and annual figures from about R78 000 to R840 000 appeared for related roles). No dependable survey was found, and nothing proved how POPIA affects hiring. Status: unverified. The ethics lesson describes POPIA in general terms only and is not legal advice.

Free learning resources named in the research

Kaggle Learn offers free short courses in Python, pandas, SQL and machine learning that run in the browser (details from a third-party page, so check kaggle.com/learn). The official scikit-learn documentation was not retrieved in the search, so check it directly. Status: indicative.
