What this page is

Before building the apps, we searched the web for what South African and world farmers need software for. Searches were run on 11 November 2026. The apps use practice rules written for the exercise. Where a search gave a real figure we say so, and we mark anything we could not confirm as unverified.

What the searches found

  • Livestock traceability and vaccination records. South Africa has digital systems for animal movement and foot-and-mouth vaccination records, and commercial herd software tracks withdrawal periods. Sources: LITS SA, Farmer's Weekly on a digital FMD platform, 3tres3 on the pork industry record system. Status: general picture only; no tool details verified.
  • Farm worker minimum wage. The national minimum wage was R28.79 an hour from 1 March 2025 (gov.za statement). One secondary source gave R30.23 from March 2026. Unverified. The Seasonal Wages app uses R28.79 as a practice figure.
  • UIF rates and seasonal worker access. Sources found were dated or unclear. Unverified. The practice rule is 1 percent each side.
  • Market agent commission. Agents typically charge 5 to 7.5 percent of the gross sale, and one market charges a fixed 5 percent market fee (Joburg Market, Durban Fresh Produce Market). Used in the Market app as practice figures.
  • Irrigation water use. Crop water use is the reference evaporation times a crop factor (FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56). Used in the Irrigation app.
  • Fertiliser rates. A Grain SA example suggests roughly 20 to 25 kg of nitrogen per tonne of target maize yield and about 15 kg N, 3 kg P and 4 kg K removed per tonne (Grain SA). The undated article should be checked against current guidance. The practice rule is 22 kg of nitrogen per tonne.
  • Spray intervals. The ideas of pre-harvest and re-entry intervals and of the most restrictive interval for a tank mix come from general pesticide-label guidance, mostly from outside South Africa (for example Pesticide Stewardship). The product label always decides.
  • Export cold chain. The PPECB audits cold chain and certifies export fruit (Farmer's Weekly). We could not retrieve official temperature set points, so the apps use invented practice ranges. Unverified.
  • Grants, loans and insurance. Land Bank and the department run a Blended Finance Scheme that pairs grants with loans, and Land Bank Insurance Company sells crop cover (SAnews). We did not find a claims procedure, so every claim rule in the app is invented practice. Unverified.

Figures we could not confirm

  • VAT is 15 percent in South Africa. The R1 000 000 registration limit in the Books app is a practice figure; check the tax authority.
  • Tax rates, thresholds and due dates in the apps are invented for the exercise.
  • Withdrawal periods, waiting times and cold room temperatures are invented for the exercise.

Use the real product label, programme document or an adviser for any real decision.
