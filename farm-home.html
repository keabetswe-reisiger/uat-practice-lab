Practice testing on farm software

Farm Lab is a set of pretend farm programs. Each one has small calculators a farmer might use, with mistakes hidden inside. You check the answers against the written rules, find the mistakes and learn how testers work. It is separate from the UAT Lab, so nothing here changes your UAT progress.

New here? Open Start here first. It takes about ten minutes.

How the levels work

  • Easy: 12 hidden bugs in the app you open.
  • Medium: 20 hidden bugs.
  • Hard: 33 hidden bugs, with the reasons and steps hidden until you ask.

Every level has a hint after your first mismatch, and you need to find 80 percent of the bugs to pass. You choose the level on the All apps page.
