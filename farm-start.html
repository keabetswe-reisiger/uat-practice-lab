What you will do

In plain words: a tester checks that software gives the right answer. You get a calculator that claims to work something out, such as how much feed a herd needs. You work the answer out yourself from the written rule, press Calculate, and compare. If they differ, you found a bug.

Coffee shop example: a till says 3 flat whites at R38 cost R104. You do the sum: 3 times 38 is R114. The till is wrong, so you report it. That is exactly this job.

A worked example

Open Herd Records, then the Weights and growth tab, then Average daily gain.

  1. The written rule says: average daily gain is the end weight minus the start weight, divided by the days.
  2. Type start 250, end 310 and days 60.
  3. Work it out yourself first: 60 kg gained in 60 days is exactly 1 kg a day.
  4. Press Calculate and read the answer.
  5. If the screen shows 0.98, the days were counted as 61. That is a bug.

In the lab you type what you saw into the test panel and the app marks you.

Words you will meet

Expected resultWhat the written rule says the answer must be.
Observed resultWhat the screen really shows. Read it exactly.
BugA difference between the expected and the observed result.
Control testA test where nothing is wrong. Passing it is the correct result.
BoundaryThe exact edge of a rule, such as exactly 30 cows. Bugs love edges.
False alarmReporting a bug that is not there. Too many fail you.
LevelEasy, Medium or Hard: how many bugs are hidden.

Try it yourself

  1. Open All apps and choose Easy.
  2. Open any app, for example Farm Shop and Delivery.
  3. Do the steps of test 1 and type what you see.
  4. Use the Extra info button to read the rules.
  5. Finish and read your result. Then try Medium.

These rules are practice rules, not farming advice. Never use them for real farm decisions.
